Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the personal-use PaperCal planner handles Google Calendar information.

Information accessed

When Google Calendar sync is enabled, PaperCal requests permission through Google OAuth to access calendar information necessary to display and synchronize events. This may include calendar names, event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, and event identifiers. It also uses OAuth authorization credentials to make authorized API requests.

How information is used

Calendar information is used to show schedules in the planner and, when you request it or enable the feature, to create or update calendar events. Information is not used for advertising or sold.

Storage and services

Depending on the user's setup, PaperCal may keep a local event cache, pending edits, and OAuth credentials on the Mac and/or reMarkable device used to operate it. A calendar PDF may be stored in the user's reMarkable cloud account when that synchronization workflow is enabled. Google processes calendar data through its APIs. The GitHub Pages service hosting this information page may process ordinary website access logs under its own policies.

Sharing

PaperCal does not sell or distribute Google Calendar information to advertisers or third-party data brokers. Data is exchanged with Google Calendar to provide sync, and may be placed in the user's configured reMarkable cloud documents as described above.

Retention, control, and deletion

Users can revoke PaperCal's Google permissions from their Google Account's third-party connections. Local PaperCal caches and credentials can be removed from the devices on which they are stored. Any generated calendar PDFs or notes already stored in a reMarkable account must be managed separately in that account. Revoking OAuth access does not automatically delete previously generated PDF copies.

Security

Access to Google Calendar uses OAuth authorization. Users should keep devices and stored authorization credentials secure. This public information website does not ask visitors to submit Google passwords or OAuth tokens.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change if PaperCal's features or data handling change. An updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
