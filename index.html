Calendar planning,
made for paper.

PaperCal is a personal calendar planner designed for reMarkable tablets. It brings daily, weekly, and monthly views together with handwritten notes and optional Google Calendar synchronization.

Personal-use project

What PaperCal does

  • Displays calendar events in daily, weekly, and monthly planner views.
  • Provides writing space for handwritten planning and notes.
  • Uses Google Calendar authorization, when enabled, to read events and send changes you initiate.
  • Can generate calendar PDF documents for a reMarkable workflow.

Google Calendar integration

Google Calendar access is optional and is requested through Google's OAuth permission screen. Permission can be revoked through your Google Account settings. See the privacy policy for more information about the use of calendar data.

Project status

PaperCal is currently a private personal-use project, not a public consumer service. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or reMarkable.
